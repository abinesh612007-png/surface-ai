Important Operator Notice: Binary Normal/Defect Classification

All six NEU-CLS categories represent specific defect categories. The dataset does not contain an explicitly labeled "normal / defect-free" category. As a consequence, this system accurately identifies the most probable defect profile among the 6 supported classes, but should be verified by a certified quality inspector for high-consequence aerospace or automotive applications.
